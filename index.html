Vein Finder

Point the camera at skin in a dim room. The app boosts the dark vessel pattern under the surface and marks it in green.

Visualization aid only, not a medical device. Phone cameras usually block near-infrared light, so results depend on your camera and lighting. For best results, use an 850 nm LED lamp and a camera without an IR-cut filter. Video stays on your device.
